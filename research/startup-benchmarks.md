# Startup benchmarks for Track 1 (Autonomous Agents)

The goal is to take a funded startup whose model is proven, build a **small MVP in 9 hours**, and add a **local twist** so it scores on Innovation. A straight clone loses points.

A funding round works as pitch evidence: "Investors put $25M into this exact problem in the US. Nobody solves it for Hungary."

---

## 1. Pine AI: an agent that phones companies for you ⭐ best fit
- **What it does:** an autonomous call agent that phones customer service for consumers. It disputes bills, cancels subscriptions, gets refunds and handles complaints. It charges a 10–30% tip on savings.
- **Traction:** $25M Series A (Dec 2025). Pine claims a 93–94% negotiation success rate and about 270 minutes saved per user (company figures). [CrowdFund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2025/12/256231-ai-agent-pine-raises-25m-series-a/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/06/pine-ai-aims-consumer-ai-agent-complex-customer-service-interactions/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pine-ai-your-ai-call-agent/id6746403769)
- **Our twist:** "Pine for people who don't speak Hungarian." International students and expats in Debrecen give a task in English, and the agent **calls a Hungarian clinic, landlord or office in Hungarian**, then reports back.
- **MVP:** one task type (book a doctor or report a repair), a live outbound call, an English summary, and approval before confirming.

## 2. Beside / Newo: an AI receptionist for small businesses
- **What it does:** answers the business's calls, remembers customers, books appointments and handles follow-ups.
- **Traction:** Beside raised $32M (Series A, Nov 2025). Newo raised $25M (Series A, Feb 2026). [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/11/11/beside-ai-voice-startup-raises-32-million-ai-receptionist-for-small-business/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/10/newo-lands-25m-bring-production-ready-ai-receptionists-small-businesses/)
- **Our twist:** a **Hungarian-speaking** receptionist for 1–3 person Debrecen businesses (barbers, dentists, tutors) that miss calls while working, and that also answers foreign students in English.
- **MVP:** an inbound phone number that answers, checks a calendar, books a slot and texts a confirmation.

## 3. Turnout / Casium: agents that handle government paperwork
- **What it does:** Turnout's agent automates about 60% of government application tasks, with professionals checking the key steps. Casium autonomously gathers information and prepares work-visa filings.
- **Traction:** Turnout raised $21M (seed, Sep 2025). Casium raised $5M (seed, 2025). [Citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/746133/shine-capital-and-lgvp-co-led-21m-seed-funding-for-turnout/), [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2025/ai2-incubator-spinout-casium-raises-5m-to-simplify-work-visa-filings/)
- **Our twist:** **Hungarian residence permits and accommodation reporting** for UniDeb's ~7,800 international students. Take a photo of the letter, the agent explains it, fills the form, sets the deadline and drafts the email.
- **MVP:** upload a document or letter, get a structured explanation, a pre-filled form and calendar reminders.

## 4. CodeRabbit / Greptile: an AI code review agent
- **What it does:** reviews every pull request automatically and catches bugs in AI-written code.
- **Traction:** CodeRabbit raised a $60M Series B (Sep 2025) and claims 8,000+ paying organisations. Greptile raised a $25M Series A from Benchmark. [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/09/16/coderabbit-gets-60m-fix-ai-generated-code-quality/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/09/23/greptile-bags-25m-funding-take-coderabbit-graphite-ai-code-validation/)
- **Our twist:** hard to find. It's crowded, and you'd need a niche, for example a reviewer for students' university assignments that explains mistakes instead of fixing them.
- **MVP:** a GitHub app that comments on PRs. Easy to build, but a weak Innovation score.

## 5. IrisGo / Anchor: an agent that learns a task by watching you once
- **What it does:** watches you do a task once, then repeats it autonomously.
- **Traction:** IrisGo raised $2.8M (seed, led by Andrew Ng's AI Fund, with Nvidia and Google). Anchor raised a $6M seed. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/irisgo-lands-28m-to-build-observational-pc-automation-agent), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/10/22/anchor-secures-6m-build-next-generation-agentic-browser-automation/)
- **Our twist:** university admin staff who repeat the same Neptun or Excel task hundreds of times.
- **MVP:** record browser steps on one demo site, then replay them over the rows of a spreadsheet. Very impressive, but the riskiest to build in 9h.
